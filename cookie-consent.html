Cookies Policy

This explains how Kingsbury Personnel Ltd uses cookies on this website. For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Small text files placed on your device when you visit our site. They help the site work properly, remember your preferences, and show us how the site is used.

How we use them

We only set non-essential cookies with your consent, in line with UK GDPR and PECR. You can change your choice anytime via Cookie Settings.

Strictly Necessary

Required for the site to function, security, load balancing, remembering your consent choice. Cannot be switched off.

Analytics

Helps us understand how visitors use the site (e.g. Google Analytics) so we can improve it. Only set with your consent.

Marketing

Used by services like Facebook and LinkedIn to measure our campaigns and show relevant content. Only set with your consent.

Third parties

Some cookies come from services on our pages, social platforms, WhatsApp, and analytics tools, governed by their own policies.

How long they last

Session cookies clear when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain until they expire or you delete them.

Managing cookies

Use Cookie Settings in the footer anytime, or manage cookies through your browser. Blocking some cookies may affect site functionality.
